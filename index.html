About

01

I work at the intersection of relativistic astrophysics and computation — building and applying general relativistic hydrodynamic (GRHD) simulations, structural jet models, and multi-wavelength statistical analysis to understand how black holes accrete, launch jets, and occasionally disrupt stars. My interests span AGN jets and jet launching, black hole accretion flows, tidal disruption events, and the electromagnetic and neutrino signatures that connect them to multimessenger astronomy. I'm currently completing my BS-MS dual degree in Physics at IISER Kolkata, and beginning my master's thesis at the National Centre for Radio Astrophysics (NCRA), Pune.

Research Experience

02
AUG 2026 — PRESENT
Linking γ-Ray Flares to Jet Structural Evolution in Blazars
Master's Thesis · Supervised by Prof. Preeti Kharb, National Centre for Radio Astrophysics (NCRA), Pune
Studying multi-wavelength and VLBI signatures of magnetic reconnection-driven γ-ray flares in blazar jets. Combining statistical analysis of 18-year Fermi-LAT light curves (skewness / state-transition tests around large flares) with MOJAVE VLBA archival data on jet component ejections, multi-wavelength light curves (VLBA, VLA, GMRT), and a magnetic plasmoid-merging simulation in PLUTO, comparing structural evolution between FSRQs and BL Lac objects.
JUN 2026 — PRESENT
Little Red Dots as Delayed Choked-Jet Tidal Disruption Events
Supervised by Prof. Mukul Bhattacharya, DAASE, IIT Indore
Investigating whether Little Red Dots — a population of compact, high-redshift (z ≈ 4–8) JWST sources with debated physical origin — can be explained within the delayed choked-jet TDE framework. Built a dynamical jet-cocoon-debris model to determine when a jet breaks out versus stays trapped, as a function of black hole mass, jet luminosity, and launch delay. Predicted high-energy neutrino and forward/reverse-shock synchrotron signatures and tested detectability against IceCube, JWST/NIRCam, VLA, ALMA, and Chandra sensitivities.
6–24 JUL 2026
Study of Martian Atmospheres: Winds and Wave Characterization
Supervised by Pedro Machado, Francisco Brasil, João Dias — Instituto de Astrofísica e Ciências do Espaço, Lisbon
Worked on the Mars component of a joint Venus–Mars atmospheric gravity wave study using OMEGA/Mars Express spectral cube data and HRSC imagery. Used omegapy and QGIS to detect and characterize wave packets and background wind fields from cloud tracking. Contributed to a group presentation on morphological characterization and wind-speed measurements.
JUN 2025 — DEC 2025
General Relativistic Hydrodynamic Simulation Code
Supervised by Dr. Sudip K. Garain, CESSI, IISER Kolkata
Solved the relativistic hydrodynamic equations analytically for an ideal equation of state, and implemented the conserved-to-primitive variable conversion (D, S_j, τ → ρ, v_j, P) in code. Developed a GRHD code for studying advective, sub-Keplerian accretion flow onto black holes for different equations of state.

Supervised by Dr. Sudip K. Garain, CESSI, IISER Kolkata
Derived the equations of motion for a test mass in a binary star system and simulated its trajectory using RK45 integration in Python, analyzing the stable and unstable Lagrange equilibrium points.
